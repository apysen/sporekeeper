# Changelog: 2026-08-27

## Cane, Adaptations, Evolutions, and relic expansion (in progress)

Implementation began from a full cross-check of the live progression, combat,
territory, drafting, and relic systems against
`Sporekeeper_Cane_Weapon_Rework.md` and `Sporekeeper_New_Adaptations.md`.

- The new universal weapon is being built as **Cane**, a 24-damage automatic melee
  sweep. It snapshots the player's facing, covers a fixed 90-degree frontal sector,
  hits every crossed enemy once, and alternates left-to-right/right-to-left.
- The three universal Thornshot upgrade lines and projectile-specific starter-weapon
  assumptions are being removed. Existing universal relics that depended on targets
  or Pierce are being translated into meaningful Cane rules rather than left inert.
- Progression is being expanded for **Thistle**, **Vine**, and **Fruit**, their twelve
  five-rank upgrade lines, all eighteen pair Evolutions, and nine path relics.
- Fruit uses the six supplied watermelon, apple, peach, orange, lemon, and strawberry
  paintings through rolling, planting, and bursting. `fruitseed.png` is reserved
  for the legendary Dropped Seed relic.
- A generated neutral watercolor-granulation map was added at
  `assets/art/shared/watercolor_granulation.png`. A shared sprite shader will apply it
  to Bloom, normal Seed, Fruitseed, and all Fruit paintings without redrawing or
  changing their silhouettes.

This entry will be extended as each runtime stage and its validation lands.

### Cane foundation completed

- Thornshot's three progression IDs, modifiers, target collection, default projectile
  firing, Pierce behavior, and starter-weapon references were removed from live code.
- Cane now attacks on a regular **0.72s** baseline cadence for **24 damage**, snapshots
  facing, and resolves every enemy intersecting a **90-degree**, **92px** sector once.
  Its visible stroke alternates direction every automatic attack.
- The attack is an area sweep, not a disguised target list: it executes even into
  empty space and never allocates a projectile. A bounded set of sweep/hit signals is
  available for Vine Evolutions without letting their secondary attacks recurse.
- The five universal relic rules that assumed projectiles were reworked: Predator's
  Eye extends every 10th sweep by 35%; Follow Through banks +35% damage after a Cane
  kill; Blood in the Water adds 25% damage below 20% health; Underdog shortens Cane
  cooldown 30% while eight enemies are within 190px; Adrenal Response now names Cane
  sweeps explicitly.
- Progression now contains Thistle, Vine, and Fruit; all twelve path lines; all
  eighteen new pair Evolutions; and the claim-as-rank-1/four-purchase rank ceiling
  required by the adaptation specification.

### Adaptation runtime and art pass completed

- Added the bounded `PhaseBAdaptationDirector` runtime and wired it into reset,
  progression updates, pausing, and status feedback.
- Thistle now plants 2-5 supported turrets from branch diversity and implements the
  **12-damage** base thorn, Clustering, Dehiscence, Burr, Needlepoint, all seven
  Thistle-pair Evolutions, and Seedhead, Phyllotaxis, and Crown of Thorns.
- Vine now applies Elongation, Lignification, Turgor, and Apical Dominance directly
  to Cane and implements THORNWHIP, MYCELIAL VINE, WISTERIA, BRIAR CANE, HEARTWOOD,
  ESPALIER, GOURDVINE, Recurve, Trellis, and Twining.
- Fruit now releases for **36 collision damage**, a **300px/s** starting velocity,
  and a **320px** unobstructed base roll. Heavy collision momentum, obstacle bounces,
  immature Green trails, terminal Fruit growth, all six Fruit-pair Evolutions, and Thick Rind,
  Bank Shot, and Dropped Seed are implemented with capped pools and capped timed
  ground zones.
- Added Rot's temporary Spore-rate trail zones and Durian's temporary Ensnare-rate
  trail zones to combat. Overlapping stamps refresh bounded regions rather than
  stacking or growing without limit.
- The six supplied Fruit paintings are used by the live Fruit system, while the
  Fruitseed painting is restricted to Dropped Seed. A generated neutral granulation texture is now applied through
  `botanical_art_watercolor.gdshader` to Bloom, Seed, Fruitseed, and Fruit art while
  preserving source geometry and bypassing non-raster telegraphs.

### Clover machine expanded to eight Path faces

- Added Thistle, Vine, and Fruit to the slot grammar, uniform Path jackpot pool,
  wild resolution, Triple Clover payout, symbol naming, reel coloring, and
  symbol-to-Path mapping.
- Added matching white-silhouette Thistle, Vine, and Fruit SVG reel icons in
  `assets/art/slots/symbols/`.
- New Adaptation jackpots lend all four lines at rank 5 for 10 seconds and fire an
  immediate readable event: a Thistle network volley, a Vine sweep, or a full Fruit
  release. Triple Clover now includes all eight Paths.

### Vine and Fruit targeting/terminal revision completed

- Base Cane still follows the player's facing. Once adapted into Vine, each automatic
  sweep instead chooses the targetable 90-degree sector containing the most enemies;
  wall-blocked and out-of-reach enemies do not count. Equal sectors prefer the nearer
  cluster and then the player's facing, preserving deterministic playback.
- Vine's live sweep, shaft, tip, and sector telegraph are now green. Vine jackpots use
  the same dense-sector targeting rule.
- Every normal Fruit release independently aims toward the densest targetable enemy
  sector from its launch point, including Orchard and detached Gourdvine launches.
- Fruit now begins at **300px/s** and follows constant physical deceleration. Enemy
  impacts and obstacle bounces reduce actual velocity; distance no longer despawns a
  Fruit, and it stops only when velocity reaches zero.
- A stopped Fruit remains on the ground with its original Fruit painting for a
  **0.5s** fuse, then bursts into territory for up to **10 base damage** with standard
  radial falloff. The ordinary Fruit never swaps to the Fruitseed painting.
- Fruit's base cooldown is now **1.0s**, beginning only after every normal Fruit and
  fragment from the previous release has exploded. Legendary Dropped Seed rolls do
  not hold that base cooldown open.

### Spec audit and reference synchronization

- Corrected three pair-Evolution mismatches found during the source audit:
  BURSTBLOOM charges now remain active for their full 4s/6s duration instead of
  being consumed by one volley; ROOTSTOCK counts every third Heavy Thorn across the
  network rather than per turret; WISTERIA blossoms now detonate sequentially in
  0.10s steps and calculate the terminal blossom's 70%-Force radius correctly.
- MYCELIAL VINE's Fungal Snap now requires the enemy to have been Overgrown before
  the Apical hit, matching “hit against an Overgrown enemy.” Root Potency now
  amplifies Vine launched from Mature Green, alongside its existing Thistle/Fruit
  integration.
- Separated Fruit collision damage from terminal explosion damage. Ripening and Root
  scale both; Graft, Prize Fruit, Bank Shot, Durian, and first-hit bonuses remain
  collision-only as authored.
- Replaced every stale Thornshot smoke-test reference with Cane sector, occlusion,
  dense Vine aim, Predator's Eye reach, and Blood in the Water damage checks.
- Rebuilt `docs/upgrade_paths_reference.md` around all **8 Paths**, all **28 pair
  Evolutions**, complete base behavior, and explicit damage/zero-damage baselines.
  Rebuilt `docs/relics.md` for all **53 relics**, including all nine new Path relics
  and exact inherited damage packets.
- Updated the Cane/adaptation design notes and active prototype/architecture docs for
  Vine auto-targeting, Fruit's planted terminal burst, the Fruitseed relic-only art,
  the eight-Path draft, and watercolor-treated botanical assets.

### Final adaptation polish continued

- Replaced frame-end Fruit trail stamps with independent segment sampling on every
  rolling Fruit. Multi-Fruit releases now paint every trajectory continuously, even
  when frame time varies or several Fruits launch from the same point.
- Added orange, lemon, and strawberry to the live Fruit painting pool alongside
  watermelon, apple, and peach. Each spawned Fruit now chooses its painting from the
  six-asset set with the run-seeded gameplay RNG; planted terminal Fruit keeps the
  same painting, while Fruitseed remains exclusive to Dropped Seed.
- Strengthened the shared Bloom/Seed/Fruit watercolor material from subtle grain to
  visibly pooled pigment, paper lift, and irregular translucent edges. All six Fruit
  paintings, planted Fruit, Bloom, ordinary Seed, and Dropped Seed use the same
  generated granulation texture.
- Replaced stretched `StyleBoxTexture` button rendering at runtime with true
  `NinePatchRect` border children. Corners remain fixed and both edge axes tile,
  eliminating the slight wide-button stretch while preserving hover, pressed,
  disabled, text, and input behavior.
- Corrected integration gaps found during the rank audit: Fruit obstacle bounces now
  retain their reflected position; terminal Fruit ground receives Rot/Durian ground
  zones; Overripe fragments retain terminal Bloom effects without re-splitting;
  Orchard Saplings wait for their queued foothold; Espalier's second fan requires
  full Charge; Hedge Battery targets the authored other in-range turret; and
  Thornwhip rank 5 fires in two timed sweep halves.
- Preserved the intentionally authored **24-damage** Cane baseline and made it the
  canonical default in code, the live scene, and every damage reference/calculation.
- Tightened ROOTSTOCK to its authored target classes: Deep Chamber now accelerates
  only on fully Mature owned Green, and Heavy Thorn punch-through stops on elites
  and bosses after piercing up to two normal enemies. Fruit integration now clamps
  motion to the exact zero-velocity time on long frames and samples every trail
  interval instead of dropping stamps behind a per-frame cap.
- Extended the smoke suite around the final pass: it now verifies every Button's
  tiled `NinePatchRect`, the strong shared botanical shader and all six Fruit assets,
  dense Fruit aim, independent three-way trails, run-seeded art variation, planted
  10-damage germination, post-explosion cooldown timing, all eight reel icons and
  mappings, and the immediate Thistle/Vine/Fruit jackpot events. The Clover master
  guide now documents all eight symbols and all three new jackpot rules.
- Kept the test fixtures aligned with the finished rules: Cane feedback is observed
  on a deliberately durable Hunter while retaining the 24-damage baseline; Vine and
  Fruit density fixtures no longer place a fourth enemy on the winning sector edge;
  Root Network coverage now uses an actually selected Root path; and presentation
  controls are exercised through their dedicated pause-settings page.
- Corrected Bloom's wet-wall response during the final smoke pass. A contact strong
  enough to leave a face splat now spends its normal velocity on that surface and
  carries only its tangential motion onward, so paint runs along and pools against
  basalt instead of visibly marking the rock and then rebounding into open ground.
- Closed the remaining adaptation/relic audit gaps: ROOTSTOCK's rank-5 network salvo
  now uses the same normal-enemy-only Heavy Thorn punch-through as rank 3, and
  Phyllotaxis's five-thorn packet visibly fans through a 48-degree spiral around the
  turret's current aim instead of stacking every bonus thorn on one trajectory.
- Split Fruit trail area from terminal foothold area. Prize Fruit's +50% area now
  applies only to the terminal Green exactly as authored, while Graft/Germination
  still scale both. Dropped Seed receives its 50% area before its first trail stamp,
  eliminating the single full-size origin mark, and Overripe fragments preserve the
  corrected terminal area when they split.
