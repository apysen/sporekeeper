# 2026-09-02

## Four-Adaptation progression

Implemented `docs/sporekeeper_adaptation_restructure_implementation_guide.md`.

- **Roster.** A run permanently owns four Adaptations: two Cores, then two
  Branches. `PhaseBProgression` holds them as `core_adaptations` and
  `branch_adaptations`, and every ownership question in the codebase goes through
  `owns_adaptation`. `primary_path` / `secondary_path` / `tertiary_path` survive as
  compatibility aliases over those arrays.
- **Acquisition is unchanged: a run enters an Adaptation by drafting one of its
  lines.** The 2026-08-30 rule stands. There is no Adaptation chooser and no screen
  of bare Adaptations at any point; the first upgrade still arrives a few kills
  into wave one, and taking a line of an Adaptation the run has not entered is what
  enters it. Taking that line starts the base mechanic, opens all four ordinary
  lines at rank 0, and unlocks the relic pool in the same moment.
- **What the milestones grant is room, not an Adaptation.** Both Core slots are
  open from the first second. The Elite's defeat opens the third slot and the
  Boss's the fourth, and each opening is paid out as a draft of real upgrade lines
  drawn only from Adaptations the run has not entered — the same shape the old
  support offer had. Between those milestones, with every open slot filled, drafts
  narrow to the Adaptations the run holds.
- **Ranks.** Ordinary lines cap at **3**, with per-rank values unchanged -- rank 3
  is sixty per cent of the former rank-5 total, not the same total in fewer steps.
  The Evolution line keeps its authored five ranks. Bramble's Entanglement, Ensnare
  Rate and potency per-rank values were corrected to the guide's table.
- **Evolution.** Frozen from the unordered Core pair the moment Core 2 lands, held
  as a symmetric 28-entry matrix in `PhaseBEvolutions.PAIR_EVOLUTIONS` so the
  draft can name it on a card before it is owned. Readiness requires two mastered lines in
  *each* Core; Branch lines, Clover ranks and relic bonuses never count.
- **Cultivation Focus.** One owned Adaptation holds slot 1 of every ordinary offer
  while it has an open line. Slots 2 and 3 are drawn from the whole candidate pool
  against drought weights, with a line unseen for four screens forced onto the
  table. Focus costs nothing, has no combat effect, and clears when its Adaptation
  is mastered.
- **Relics.** Eligible pools are Plant plus one per owned Adaptation, so the
  catalogue grows 16 → 24 → 32 → 40. The rarity table now steps on owned count
  (`RunState.SUPPORT_LATE` became `FULL_ROSTER`). An exhausted pool pays a Compost
  Cache -- 20% health and 0.75x a baseline Seed's Fresh Green -- rather than
  Biomass.
- **Removed.** The support/tertiary slot and its upgrade-card acquisition,
  `upgrade_slot_count` and `evolution_bonus_upgrade_slots`, Evolution granting
  extra slots, the exhausted-draft Biomass payout (now a one-shot
  `garden_completed`), and the Clover overlay's ability to raise Thistle's
  permanent turret cap.
- **HUD.** Five rows -- Core 1, Core 2, Evolution, Branch 1, Branch 2 -- each
  showing all four lines, a bought line reading `rank/maximum` and an unbought one
  keeping only its marker, plus a Focus marker and a brightened accent at mastery.
  Unearned Branch rows show a lock naming the milestone that opens them.

## 25-wave Campaign

Implemented `docs/sporekeeper_campaign_implementation_guide.md`.
`docs/campaign_enemy_scaling.md` predates it and is obsolete.

- **Structure.** 25 waves of 60 seconds. Elite Rootbreaker at Wave 6 second 35,
  Burrower Boss at Wave 13 after its opening cue, ten relic opportunities (waves 3,
  6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24 plus both milestone defeats), reinforcements cut at
  24:45, victory checked at 25:00.
- **Data.** `CampaignWaveSpec` and `EnemyRoleScalingProfile` Resources, with the
  whole authored schedule built in `PhaseBCampaignWaves`. Wave-number branches are
  gone from the director.
- **Six curves, fixed per wave.** Health 1.10→3.60, contact damage 1.05→2.25,
  movement 1.00→1.15, territory strength 1.10→3.20, territory action speed
  1.00→1.22, plus CC resistance (0% until Wave 9, 20% at Wave 25) and modifier
  chance (0% until Wave 4, 70% at Wave 25). Enemies are built from their wave's
  fixed numbers at spawn, not from continuous run time.
- **Role exceptions.** Movement and action-speed shares are authored per role: the
  Rusher takes half the movement curve, the Boss less; Grazers keep their 0.14s
  bite cadence and scale only the rest between bursts; contact intervals never
  scale at all.
- **Crowd control.** Root duration, shove distance and slow intensity are reduced
  by the wave's resistance, resolved once per body at spawn. Entanglement build-up
  is deliberately untouched, so Bramble still works at Wave 25 and only its payoff
  is trimmed.
- **Scheduler.** A bounded debt scheduler replaces at-most-one-pack-per-frame, so
  requested density is what the clock says rather than what the frame rate allowed.
  Ordinary packs stop at 124 active actors, leaving six slots for the milestones
  and Colony Cores; a rejected ordinary request is recorded and discarded, and
  milestone requests retry.
- **Composition.** A 100-entry leader deck per wave, shuffled from the run seed, so
  authored proportions hold without streaks. Followers are chosen by leader role
  with caps on duplicate specialists.
- **Topology.** `PhaseBSpawnTopology` scores eight perimeter sectors four times a
  second on Green coverage, maturity, live enemies and remembered player damage,
  and serves eight authored placement profiles. Ordinary spawns keep 220px from
  Grandma, Rushers 280px.
- **Modifiers.** Unlock in four stages by wave, gated per role, with Wave 18
  steering its first eligible spawns to demonstrate three different rule changes.

## Player damage feedback

- The Gardener flashes pure white for 0.15s on a hit, then blinks for the rest of
  the invincibility window.
- A red screen flash scaled to the hit's severity, fading on a squared curve.
- A 0.04s freeze of the whole simulation on a heavy hit only.
- Damage taken by the player spawns a distinct violet, critical-styled number, so
  the one number that threatens the run is findable inside the ones that do not.
