# 2026-09-04

## Thistle is one turret you carry, not a field you forget

Thistle was the cheapest damage in the game: two to five permanent turrets grew on
owned Green, replanted themselves forever, and asked nothing of the player after
the draft. It is now a single turret with a real thistle's defining habit — it
catches on whatever walks through it.

- **One permanent turret, always.** `_get_thistle_capacity` returns 1. The four
  Thistle lines no longer buy turret slots.
- **Carrying.** Walk Grandma within **15 px** of the turret and it sticks to her for
  **3s**, riding along and firing from wherever she is. Supporting Green is not
  evaluated while it is carried — it is on her coat, not in the ground — so it
  shoots happily over bare dirt for the length of the ride.
- **Two rules stop it being a free mobile turret.** It will not catch again for
  **7s** after a drop, and if it works loose while Grandma is off supporting Green it
  **withers** instead of replanting. Carrying it somewhere useful is the decision;
  dropping it badly costs the turret.
- **Losing the turret costs 5 seconds.** However it goes — an enemy taking the Green
  out from under it, or a bad drop — the plot stays empty for
  `thistle_replant_seconds`, now **5.0s**, before a replacement grows. It used to
  reappear the same frame, which meant losing it cost nothing. This also finally
  gives that long-declared, never-read export a job.
- **The four lines buy carry instead of slots.** Each started line adds **+0.25s**
  to the ride and takes **−0.55s** off the wait between rides: all four carry for 4s
  on a 4.8s cooldown. Spreading across lines still pays; it just pays in mobility.
- **Temporary turrets are untouched.** Burdock's planted heads and Deadhead Flush's
  pair go through `_plant_thistle` directly and never consulted the cap, so they
  still stack on top of the permanent one. Nerfing Thistle was not meant to nerf
  Fruit or Pruning by accident.

## The Thistle relic pool survives the cap

Three of the pool's eight relics were written for a network and would have read as
dead text against a single turret. `_find_network_responder` now falls back to the
source turret when it is the only one standing, at `SOLO_NETWORK_SHARE` (65%).

- **Thorn Relay** and **Answering Thorn** answer themselves at 65% of their listed
  share rather than doing nothing.
- **Crossfire** (HEDGE BATTERY rank 5) does the same. A second turret still wins the
  tie whenever one exists.
- **Crown of Thorns** has nothing to synchronise with at a network of one, so the
  sole Thistle fires **3 volleys 0.14s apart** instead of a single ordinary one.
- **Rosette** needed no change and quietly became the pool's best relic: a turret
  that takes root is a new turret however it got there, so it now pays out on every
  drop rather than once a run. The stick cooldown is what bounds it.

## Root Potency now reaches all four Thistle lines

Root's amplification reached Thistle through the generic virtual-rank path in
`_effective_upgrade`, which is invisible, gated on the turret standing on Mature
ground, and pays every line in the same currency. Thistle now takes it in authored
form instead, so what each line gets is the thing that line is about:

| Line | Root Potency adds |
|---|---|
| Clustering | +1 thorn in the volley |
| Burr | +1 ricochet on every thorn |
| Dehiscence | attack rate × Root's own amplification |
| Needlepoint | critical chance × that same amplification |

Counts get whole units; rates get the multiplier every other Root consumer already
reads (`get_network_multiplier_here`, +25%/rank over a +30% base in the scene, scaled
by ground maturity). The four Thistle ids are excluded from `_effective_upgrade`'s
rank bonus so nothing double-dips, and the gate is unchanged: Mature player ground
under the turret, and a line the run has actually started. Thorn damage is still not
amplified — that was removed deliberately and stays removed.

Worth knowing: this applies at the turret's position, so a carried Thistle is
amplified by whatever Grandma is standing on, which gives the carry loop a second
reason to care where you put it down.

## Experience collection

- Attraction radius **165 px → 82 px**, now the `xp_attraction_radius_pixels` export
  rather than a literal. Walking the field is part of collecting again.
- The pull-in ramp was a second hardcoded constant (170) that would have flattened
  against a smaller radius; it is derived from the radius now.
- The distance-independent give-up timer that eventually vacuums every orb — the
  real ceiling on what a smaller radius can cost a run — is exported as
  `xp_giveup_seconds` and moved **7s → 11s**, so the radius cut is felt rather than
  refunded a moment later.

## Tests

`thistle_spacing_probe` covers the new loop end to end: one permanent turret, lines
buying carry rather than slots, catching, riding, dropping onto Green, withering off
it, and staying gone for the replant delay before regrowing. The smoke probe's Thistle
jackpot check now expects one turret rather than two, its Focus checks are replaced by
one asserting a mastered Adaptation drops out of the draw, and the Adaptation-tree
check no longer expects a Focus marker on the row. `root_new_adaptations_probe` asserts
the four Thistle lines take no virtual ranks, checks each authored effect against the
live amplification rather than a hardcoded constant, and confirms a turret off Mature
ground gets none of it.

## Cultivation Focus removed

Pulled out of the build for now. The guaranteed slot-one draw is gone, so every
ordinary offer is now three cards drawn from the unrestricted weighted pool; the
drought guards (`line_drought_boost_screens`, `line_drought_forced_screens`) are what
keep a wanted line from going missing for minutes, and they were already doing most
of that work. Removed with it: the `focused_adaptation` state and its signal,
`set_focused_adaptation` / `get_focusable_adaptations` / `_draw_focus_line`, the
default-Focus-on-Core-1 assignment, the mastery-clears-Focus path, the `FOCUS` choice
screen and its prompt, and the `◆` marker on the focused HUD row. The scene's
placeholder card on `Choice4` was a Focus card and is now an ordinary upgrade card,
so the editor still shows what the panel actually renders.

## Wave label

`WAVE 1 - GRAZER SURGE` is now just `WAVE 1`. The named-wave branch and
`wave_named_template` are gone; the label writes the scene's `WAVE {wave}` template
and nothing else. Campaign waves still carry their display names in the wave tables —
`get_wave_display_name` is intact — the HUD simply no longer prints them.

## Pruning: a full ring now says so

Charge was only ever drawn as a gradient — longer spikes, a warmer tint, a faster turn
— and a gradient has no ceiling the eye can find, so a ring at 90% looked like a ring
at 100% and there was no moment that said *release now*. A full ring now pulses: a hoop
of light standing `full_charge_hoop_offset_pixels` outside the ring plus a halo on each
spike, beating at `full_charge_pulse_hz` (2.2/s) down to `full_charge_pulse_depth`.

The pulse clock is deliberately a state, not a gradient: it is zero below full charge
and zero once the ring is spent, and it resets on the frame the ring fills so the first
beat is the brightest one — the beat the player needs to catch. It runs on the
sim-gated `advance` tick rather than wall-clock, so it holds still while paused. It is
not scaled by the Friendly Effects setting, matching the rest of the pruning director
and the rule that readability cues are not spectacle.

## Root Potency reaches the rest of Pruning

Only Cutting Edge took Root; Harvest Rate, Cutting Yield and Charge took none, in the
sim or the HUD. All four now do, measured two ways because the ring is a point and a
harvest is a patch:

- **Cutting Yield** (with Cutting Edge) reads the existing `get_harvest_root_multiplier`
  — the Mature share of the ground the cut actually took.
- **Harvest Rate** and **Charge** read a new `get_ring_root_multiplier`, the Network
  where Grandma is standing, the way every other point effect in the game does.

Worth flagging: amplifying Harvest Rate raises spikes faster **and burns Green faster**,
since the drain is the harvest. That is the same trade the line is already priced on and
`maximum_upkeep_cells_per_second` still caps it, but it does mean Root makes a Pruning
run eat its own ecosystem quicker. Easy to back out if it plays badly — it is one
factor in `get_upkeep_cells_per_second`.

## HUD: the Root Network rows were lying about Thistle

`_get_root_potency_effect` still reported "N damage per ordinary thorn" × the Network
multiplier for all four Thistle lines. Thistle thorn damage has not been amplified for
some time, and after today's change the four lines take Root in four different shapes,
so one shared damage line was wrong twice over. Each Thistle line now reports its own
authored effect, and the step lines report nothing at all when the run has not bought
them — Root deepens a started line and never invents one. Pruning's three new rows are
wired the same way.

## Thistle relic text rewritten for a one-turret network

The pool was written for a network and the descriptions read that way -- "another
Thistle", "every active Thistle", "across the network" -- which is nonsense text
against a single turret, and the qualifiers bolted on earlier today only made it
worse. All eight now lead with the single Thistle and treat a second one (Burdock's
planted head, Deadhead Flush's pair) as the exception:

- **Thorn Relay** and **Answering Thorn** quote the solo number first -- 42% and 33%,
  the listed share times `SOLO_NETWORK_SHARE` -- and name the full share as what a
  second Thistle would answer with.
- **Crown of Thorns** leads with the three quick volleys, with the synchronised
  network volley as the "if there is more than one" case.
- **Seedhead** now names the case the player will actually hit: it fires as the
  Thistle collapses, *including when you drop it off the Green*.
- **Rosette** leads on the carry loop, which is where its payout now comes from.
- **Pincushion** and **Phyllotaxis** just drop the network phrasing.

The pool's header comment in the catalog said "a distributed turret network passes
firing events around", which stopped being true this morning.

## Tests

`pruning_charge_tell_probe` is a new dedicated probe for the full-Charge tell: nothing
while filling, brightest on the frame it fills, beating between bright and its authored
dim, and nothing at all once the ring is spent. It lives on its own rather than inside
the smoke probe because the tell needs an ecosystem big enough to feed an unbroken
eight-second hold, which the smoke's fixture is not -- a ring held there strips its
footing and releases part-way, which is a fine thing for the harvest tests to prove and
a useless fixture for this one.

## Quick mode drops the ADAPTATIONS heading

The Clover cabinet sits on top of it. The four rows beneath the heading each name
their own Adaptation, so the word is the redundant half and it is what gives way --
`slots_label` is hidden whenever Clover is enabled, and the panel is not re-laid out
around the gap because the rows never moved. Campaign, which has no cabinet, keeps the
heading; the scene placeholder still reads ADAPTATIONS, which is what Campaign prints.

## HUD: the plain Pruning Charge row

`get_charge_seconds()` now folds in the Root Network where Grandma is standing, so the
ordinary (non-Root) Charge row was quietly reporting an amplified number and the Root
row beneath it was double-counting the same bonus. The plain row reads the exports
directly now, the way the Harvest Rate row already did.
