# Changelog: 2026-08-29

## HUD, run-length modes, mechanics help, performance, and adaptation fixes

This entry covers work completed after `2026-08-27.md`. It records the final
behavior of systems that went through multiple revisions rather than preserving
superseded intermediate versions.

---

## 1. The run HUD was reorganized around the upgrade concept

- Relics moved from the right-side HUD into the original bottom tray. The tray's
  original frame and footprint were restored after the first layout pass.
- The upgrade display moved to the right side beneath the slot machine and was
  renamed **ADAPTATIONS**.
- The health bar, Green/territory bar, XP bar, and slot machine retained their
  authored positions.
- Developer controls moved beside the pause menu instead of consuming permanent HUD
  space. The redundant in-run **Restart Run** button was removed; restarting remains
  available through pause.
- The Adaptations tree was cleaned up into two primary Path rows, a central Evolution,
  and a support Path row. Repeated `PATH 1`, `PATH 2`, `EVOLUTION`, and `SUPPORT PATH`
  captions, plus the redundant filled/total counter, were removed.
- The backend upgrade-line capacity now expands from the original eight paired-Path
  lines to **13 positions after Evolution**: eight existing lines, one Evolution line,
  and four support-Path lines.
- Evolution and support positions display locks until those systems are available.
  The support emblem itself begins with a lock and changes only after Evolution opens
  support selection.
- Claiming an Evolution now grants its Evolution upgrade at rank 1 immediately.
  Choosing a support reward similarly grants rank 1 of the selected support upgrade
  and derives the support Path from that upgrade, matching the opening Path choice.

### Adaptation tree art

- Added a clean circular frame matching the existing low-detail, pixel-consistent
  button and upgrade-frame language.
- Replaced Path abbreviations inside the circular emblems with centered artwork from
  `assets/art/adaptation_icons/`: Spore, Bloom, Bramble, Root, Pruning, Thistle, Vine,
  and Fruit now read visually rather than as initials.
- Kept the generated simple Root emblem after review and used the reorganized
  adaptation-icon asset folder for the other Paths.
- Added mirrored `divider-fade-003` art around the Evolution position: the source
  orientation is used on the right and its horizontal mirror on the left.

## 2. Kenney fantasy borders were rebuilt as true nine-slices

- Adopted the Kenney fantasy UI border family for the HUD and menus, including
  `panel-border-007` for the HUD treatment and `panel-border-008` for mechanics-help
  cards.
- Replaced smeared or striped `StyleBoxTexture` rendering with actual
  `NinePatchRect` children for texture-backed buttons and panels.
- Nine-slice margins now preserve one native corner square at each corner instead of
  stretching the corner horizontally or vertically. Nearest filtering keeps the
  pixel art sharp.
- The shared nine-patch treatment now covers the title frame and title buttons,
  ordinary panels and buttons, relic slots, Adaptation upgrade slots, and mechanics
  help, while retaining each button's hover, pressed, disabled, focus, text, and input
  behavior.
- Adaptation slots were deliberately simplified after the first framed pass so the
  compact tree does not read as a stack of ornate boxes.

## 3. Quick and Campaign run modes

- Added two selectable run modes. The title screen presents **CAMPAIGN** first and
  **QUICK** second without exposing durations in the button labels.
- **Quick** preserves the original 12-minute balance and XP growth curve
  (`1.34x` requirement growth per level).
- **Campaign** is a 30-minute progression with a gentler `1.20x` XP requirement
  curve, allowing the larger 13-position build to keep developing without producing
  excessively rapid late-run levels.
- Campaign schedules its first Path at 3:00, second Path at 7:30, Evolution-era boss
  pressure at 15:00, support Path at 20:00, final invasion at 25:00, and cleanup at
  28:45.
- Campaign enemy introductions and spawn density are distributed across the longer
  run. Its final spawn interval reaches `0.14s`, final enemy health reaches `3.0x`,
  and final contact damage reaches `2.0x`.
- The selected mode is applied consistently to the wave director and progression
  before a run begins.
- The run scene begins threaded loading while the title menu is open. Selecting a mode
  uses the already-loaded `PackedScene` when ready, with an explicit loading overlay
  and synchronous fallback if background loading fails.

## 4. Presentation settings were reduced to choices that can work reliably

- Compact HUD detail is now the single default presentation, and the HUD-detail
  toggle was removed.
- UI scale is fixed at the authored 100%. The ineffective scale choice was removed
  because this HUD cannot be safely reduced without sacrificing readability or
  hiding required information.
- The remaining presentation controls continue to cover meaningful battlefield
  visibility options rather than alternate HUD layouts.

## 5. Contextual mechanics help and glossary

- Added a mechanics-help system for terms that playtests showed were unclear,
  including Green, Mature, Biomass, Metabolism, Mass, Pressure, Cohesion, Overgrow,
  Entanglement, Root, Cuttings, Root Network, Root Potency, Amplification, and related
  Path mechanics.
- Help cards use a dark olive background, the slot-machine dial's gold color, and
  `panel-border-008`. Their height is content-driven, so short definitions no longer
  leave large empty boxes.
- Hovering a reward displays only the mechanics referenced by that reward in a stack
  beside the reward panel. It does not repeat the reward's full base description.
  The stack stays open while inspecting that reward and changes when another reward
  is hovered.
- Relic and Adaptation slots can be inspected during live play and while paused.
  Relic help opens directly above the bottom-tray slot; Adaptation help opens directly
  beside the right-side slot instead of drifting toward the health bar.
- Live-HUD cards remain open across the short cursor trip from a slot into its card
  and close only after the cursor leaves the combined inspection area by a reasonable
  distance.
- Keywords inside a base relic or Adaptation description can be hovered manually to
  open a second explanatory card. Further keywords can continue the stack.
- A mechanic already present anywhere in the open stack cannot be opened again, which
  prevents loops such as Green -> Metabolism -> Green.
- Keyword detection now uses complete term boundaries. Substrings no longer create
  unrelated cards; in particular, **Biomass** no longer opens **Mass**.
- Longer phrases own their component words, so **Root Potency** does not also create a
  redundant **Root** card.
- Rebuilt the pause-menu glossary as a centered, organized screen with an `ALL` tab
  and alphabetical tabs derived from the available entries. The Glossary control is
  now a properly laid-out pause-menu button rather than overlaid text.

## 6. Late-wave performance pass

- Added focused performance probes for a saturated combat/relic workload and for
  waves 10-12. The wave probe records average, p95, p99, peak frame time, enemy and
  pickup counts, and draw calls against a `16.67ms` frame budget.
- Kept frequently spawned enemies, projectiles, pickups, Briar objects, eruptions,
  traps, Fruiting Bodies, and adaptation attacks in bounded reusable pools.
- Capped simultaneously active enemies independently from the allocated enemy pool so
  spawn pressure cannot create unbounded simulation work.
- Reworked crowd navigation around shared, preallocated distance fields instead of
  running a separate path search for every enemy. Small enemies, bosses, and catalyst
  seekers share refreshed fields suited to their clearance and target.
- Navigation refreshes are throttled and staggered, and distance fields rebuild only
  when their target cell changes.
- Cane/Vine target evaluation caches obstacle-aware routes for reuse by the attack
  that follows the targeting pass.
- Territory topology, metrics, simulation, and visual refreshes remain separated by
  cadence so expensive field work does not all land on every rendered frame.
- Reused compact arrays and bounded queues in hot simulation paths to reduce transient
  allocations and late-wave garbage pressure.

These changes target a stable 60 FPS budget under the authored stress fixtures. They
reduce known CPU spikes but do not constitute a promise that every hardware,
resolution, driver, or future content combination can never exceed 16.67ms.

## 7. Pruning, Vine, and Thistle interaction fixes

- Pruning Cuttings now collide with basalt walls and rocks. A Cutting is spent when
  its swept frame segment reaches an obstacle and cannot damage an enemy through it.
- Vine attacks now calculate an obstacle-aware route around basalt. The route uses the
  same collision mask as movement and growth, removes unnecessary intermediate bends,
  and is trimmed by actual traveled length.
- Vine can hit an enemy around a wall only when the wrapped path reaches it. The visible
  Vine uses the exact same bent route as hit resolution, so the lash hugs the obstacle
  rather than visually passing through it or damaging around it invisibly.
- Thistle placement now reserves each turret's visual footprint. The initial pair,
  normal replacement planting, and temporary planting requests cannot stack turrets
  on top of an active Thistle.

## 8. Root Potency finalized for Thistle, Vine, and Fruit

- Removed the blanket Root damage/area multiplier from the three new Adaptations.
  Root Potency now strengthens each upgrade according to what that upgrade actually
  controls, matching the older five Paths:
  - Thistle: projectile count, attack speed, ricochets, and critical chance.
  - Vine: reach, damage, cooldown, and Apical Dominance damage.
  - Fruit: Fruit count, collision/terminal damage, roll distance, and germination area.
- Each purchased Root Potency rank contributes **+1 effective rank** while the effect
  originates from fully Mature owned Green. Potency rank 5 therefore contributes five
  effective ranks.
- The bonus applies only when the player owns at least rank 1 of the affected
  Adaptation line. For example, owned Burr 1 plus Potency 5 gives six ricochets, while
  owned Fruit Set 1 plus Potency 5 becomes effective rank 6 and releases seven Fruit
  total including the base Fruit.
- A Path jackpot can lend ranks to an unowned Adaptation line, but that temporary line
  does not qualify for Root Potency. A Root jackpot likewise cannot substitute for
  purchased Root Potency ranks.
- Origin checks follow the actual mechanic: Thistle snapshots its firing origin,
  Fruit snapshots its launch origin, and Vine refreshes from the player's sweep origin
  as the player moves.

  density, health, and damage configuration.
- `wave_stress_probe.gd` exercises waves 10-12 with saturated pooled combat
  entities and records percentile frame timing.
- `kenney_border_visual_probe.gd` checks native nine-slice corner sizes across menu,
  panel, relic, and Adaptation controls.
- `adaptation_emblem_visual_probe.gd` captures and verifies the eight circular Path
  emblems.
- `mechanics_help_visual_probe.gd` covers reward stacks, live and paused HUD hover,
  nested cards, duplicate prevention, exact keyword boundaries, anchor placement,
  content height, and glossary layout.
- `pruning_vine_obstacle_probe.gd` verifies Cutting obstruction and identical visible
  and damaging Vine routes around basalt.
- `thistle_spacing_probe.gd` verifies initial and requested Thistle placement spacing.
- `root_new_adaptations_probe.gd` verifies all twelve ownership-gated Potency effects,
  removal of blanket scaling, and both jackpot exclusions.

The focused Root Potency, Thistle spacing, and Pruning/Vine obstacle probes pass in
Godot 4.7.2. The mechanics-help visual probe also passes its final keyword-boundary
regression.
