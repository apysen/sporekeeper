# Changelog: 2026-08-20

## Implemented

- Added the four remaining core GDD enemy roles: Saboteur, Corruptor,
  Colonizer, and Warden.
- Added targetable Seed/Bloom durability so Saboteurs can hunt and destroy
  charging catalysts, including obstacle-aware routing to hidden targets.
- Added hostile conversion for Corruptors, targetable hostile Colony Cores for
  Colonizers, and maturity-suppression zones for Wardens.
- Kept hostile growth, suppression, and catalyst damage blocked by basalt walls.
- Added distinct procedural silhouettes, hit feedback, health display, XP
  rewards, and Inspector-editable profiles for every new role and Colony Core.
- Added staged specialist introductions and pack cadences to the twelve-minute
  wave schedule.
- Expanded smoke, territory, navigation, performance, and visual fixtures to
  cover the complete core roster.
- Updated the Phase B editing guide, component conventions, project structure,
  and territory architecture documentation for the new enemy systems.
- Added the Bramble Plant path as a selectable primary or secondary path, with
  timed two-path commitment and path-filtered upgrade drafts.
- Added Bramble's four GDD lines: Thorn Damage, Bramble Resistance,
  Entanglement, and Retaliation. Every new tuning field includes an Inspector
  hover description.
- Added an authoritative cached Green-frontier query that excludes basalt
  borders, plus repeated contact damage, movement slow, visible hit feedback,
  breach damage, and frontier-only resistance to erosion/hostile conversion.
- Added a procedural high-resolution thorn treatment to armed Green borders;
  gameplay remains field-authoritative rather than shader-driven.
- Added both Bramble pair evolutions: pooled temporary Briar Pod perimeters for
  Spore/Bramble and cooldown-limited, wall-occluded Thornburst retaliation for
  Bloom/Bramble. Spore/Bloom continues to grant Super Bloom.
- Updated the HUD, choice panel, timeline debug behavior, visual fixture,
  documentation, and tests for all three Plant path pairings.
- Expanded the dense performance fixture to include Bramble frontier queries
  and all twelve Briar Pod slots, remaining within the 16.67 ms frame budget.

## Bramble frontier development

- Added an authoritative per-cell Bramble development layer to `TerritoryField`.
  A frontier cell starts unarmed and reaches full thorn strength over roughly
  four seconds of undisturbed contact.
- Tied development speed to territory maturity, so Mature Green establishes
  Bramble up to twice as fast as freshly created territory.
- Made frontier movement reset development: a cell that stops bordering
  non-player ground decays to zero, so territory newly exposed by a Grazer
  re-enters the frontier as fresh, unstable ground.
- Made enemy erosion and hostile conversion knock development back down at the
  cells they touch, and scaled Bramble Resistance by development so a fresh
  wound is not instantly armored.
- Reduced base thorn contact damage from 5.0 to 2.0. A basic early Grazer now
  takes 9.4 seconds of continuous full-strength contact to die, falling to 4.2
  seconds at maximum Thorn Damage.
- Reduced the base Entanglement slow from 18% to 12% so damage and slow no
  longer multiply into an early automatic kill zone.
- Documented and covered the existing single-sample contact rule: thorn damage
  resolves once per enemy per tick from the strongest boundary it touches, so
  jagged or convoluted geometry cannot multiply Bramble DPS.
- Gated Retaliation and Thornburst breach events behind Established development
  and added a per-enemy Retaliation cooldown, so a rapidly eroding Grazer can no
  longer trigger retaliation many times per second.
- Encoded development into the spare range of the field texture's mask channel
  so the shader grows bare stems into sharp thorn tips as a border settles,
  without a second texture and without deciding any gameplay result.
- Hoisted the development update into the fixed-step cell loop and skipped the
  layer entirely while Bramble is unselected, keeping the dense stress fixture
  inside the 16.67 ms frame budget.

## Open work carried forward

- The Hive Queen remains a separate future boss implementation.
- Elite modifiers and the production enemy art pass remain outside this roster
  milestone.
- Bramble keeps its four original stat lines. Replacing one with a Bramble
  Growth Speed line depends on whether the growth delay proves strategically
  interesting in playtesting; `bramble_development_seconds` and
  `bramble_maturity_growth_bonus` are already Inspector tuning if it does.
